# Skincare Routine — Milia Management Protocol

## Profile
- **Skin Type:** Combination
- **Primary Concern:** Milia (localized on and around the nose)
- **Secondary Concern:** Sensitivity & barrier fragility — prioritize recovery over aggressive treatment

---

## Device: Amiro Pro
| Mode | Schedule | Status |
|------|----------|--------|
| Morning EMS | Paused | Causing discomfort — resume only after barrier recovery |
| Night RF | Tuesday only | Active — paired with hydration recovery, no acids |

## Daily Base Routine

### Morning (AM)
1. Gentle cleanser
2. Olay Essence Water
3. Olay Essence
4. Vitamin C Serum
5. Moisturizer / Olay Soft Cream
6. SPF (required daily, especially on active exfoliant nights prior)
> **EMS:** Paused until barrier discomfort resolves.

### Evening (PM) — Base Steps (applied every night before any actives)
1. Double cleanse or gentle cleanser
2. Olay Essence Water
3. Olay Essence

---

## Weekly Schedule

| Night | Treatment | Key Rule |
|-------|-----------|----------|
| **Monday** | Retinol (COSRX 0.3%) + Olay Soft Cream | Apply retinol to full face; allow 20–30 min before moisturizer |
| **Tuesday** | Amiro Night RF + Sheet Mask or Soft Cream | No acids. Hydration and device recovery only |
| **Wednesday** | Warm towel press → Juju Clay Mask → BHA (targeted) | Towel press softens sebum plugs before mask; BHA on nose/T-zone only |
| **Thursday** | Hydration recovery — Olay set only | Buffer night after BHA; no actives, focus on barrier |
| **Friday** | BHA (targeted — nose/T-zone only) | Apply to milia-prone areas; avoid rest of face |
| **Saturday** | Rest — optional sheet mask | Allow skin to recover; no actives |
| **Sunday** | BHA (targeted — nose/T-zone only) | Same protocol as Friday |

---

## Active Separation Rules
- **Retinol and BHA are never used on the same night.**
- BHA is applied only as a **spot/targeted treatment** on the nose and T-zone — never as a full-face exfoliant.
- Tuesday is locked as a **device + hydration night** — no acids regardless of schedule changes.
- Wednesday's warm towel press (2–3 min) runs before the clay mask to soften congestion and improve extraction efficacy.

---

## Milia Management Notes
- Milia on the nose region are keratin cysts, not standard acne — avoid picking or mechanical extraction.
- BHA (salicylic acid) helps dissolve the keratin plug from within; consistency over 4–8 weeks is required to see results.
- The clay mask on Wednesdays complements BHA by drawing out excess sebum and impurities before the acid application.
- If any step causes stinging, redness, or peeling, pause that active for one full week and return to hydration-only nights.

---

## Barrier Recovery Protocol (when irritation occurs)
1. Pause all actives (retinol, BHA, Vitamin C).
2. Run base routine only: Olay Essence Water → Olay Essence → Soft Cream.
3. Add a sheet mask on any night that feels tight or reactive.
4. Keep Amiro EMS paused — only RF on Tuesday if tolerated.
5. Resume actives one at a time, starting with BHA at lowest frequency (Sunday only) after skin feels calm for 5+ days.
